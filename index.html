09 / CLOSING ESSAY

THE STORY SO FAR

BUTWAL, NEPAL · DOCUMENTED IN REAL-TIME

I was born and raised in Butwal, Nepal — a vibrant junction city nestled against the green Chure hills. Growing up in a middle-class household, my world was shaped by hard work, simplicity, and the towering silhouette of the Himalayas on the northern horizon.

My introduction to computers and the internet changed everything. In a small room with quiet concentration as my closest companion, I began exploring web technologies, visual storytelling, and digital commerce. Without formal coding academies or mentors, I taught myself by reading documentation, testing prototypes, and learning from mistakes.

Alongside my passion for technology is an enduring devotion to Nepal's mountains and trails. Trekking into the high hills taught me resilience, patience, and humility. Today, at 16, I'm collaborating with friends to build digital commerce solutions, deploy reliable web servers, and document every step of my journey.

“The journey has only started.” — PRAJWAL POUDEL · 2026
